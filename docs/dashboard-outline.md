# Dashboard Outline

## Proposed sections

1. Overview
   Club selector, period selector, KPI strip, and top-level comparison chart.

2. Transfers
   Gross spend, transfer income, net spend, and manager-attributed transfer windows.

3. Results and trophies
   Points, league finish, qualification context, and trophy markers.

4. Managers
   Manager spells, handovers, and spend allocation by tenure.

5. Finance
   Revenue mix, wage bill, debt, and profitability after normalization is complete.

6. Data quality
   Coverage, source links, confidence notes, and clear gating for unfinished metrics.

## Implementation order

1. Keep the transfer explorer as the stable default.
2. Complete the performance and achievement joins.
3. Normalize finance to a comparable currency layer.
4. Populate the wage pipeline.
5. Unlock cost-efficiency and finance-heavy modules.
