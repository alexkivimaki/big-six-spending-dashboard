The Directors consider Fordstam Limited to be the ultimate parent company of the Group, and the ultimate

been drawn up is that headed by Fordstam Limited.

Companies, Companies House, Crown Way, Cardiff, CF14 3UZ.

Post balance sheet events
Acquisition and disposal of players
Since the year end the Group has acquired the registration of 8 football players at an initial cost of
£69,052,000 (2014: £62,997,000) and disposed of the registration of 6 players at a profit of £21,285,000
(2014: £19,602,000).
The Group also received £5,686,000 (2014: {nil) in respect of sell on clauses for players disposed of in
previous years.
