Factors that may affect future current and total tax charges
Reductions in the UK corporation tax rate from 23% to 21% (effective from | April 2014) and 20%
(effective from 1 April 2015) were substantively enacted on 2 July 2013. In the Budget on 8 July 2015, the
Chancellor announced additional planned reductions to 18% by 2020. This will reduce the Group's future
current tax charge accordingly. The deferred tax asset at 30 June 2015 has been calculated based on the rate
of 20% substantively enacted at the balance sheet date.
Company result for the financial year

profit and loss account. The loss for the financial year dealt with in the accounts of Chelsea FC plc was
£ 16,327,000 (2014: £161,000).
