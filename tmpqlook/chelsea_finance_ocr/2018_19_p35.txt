Scheme (the scheme'). Accrual of benefits under a final salary basis was suspended with effect from 31
August 1999 following an actuarial review, which revealed a substantial deficit.
As one of a number of participating employers, the Group is advised only of its share of the deficit in the scheme.
The latest actuarial valuation as at 31 August 2017 highlighted that the Group share of the deficit was £378,779.
