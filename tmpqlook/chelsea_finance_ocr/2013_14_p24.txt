As part of the Eurobond issue in 1997 the Company made a loan to Chelsea Pitch Owners plc of £11,151,000,
which is interest free and has an unspecified repayment date. This was used to acquire the share capital of
Chelsea Stadium Limited (previously Stardust Investments Limited) and discharge the debts of that Company in
order to leave the freehold interest in the stadium site unencumbered.
On the same date, Chelsea Stadium Limited (previously Stardust Investments Limited) granted a long leasehold
interest over the stadium site at a peppercorn rent to Chelsea Football Club Limited. Chelsea Pitch Owners plc
is obliged to repay the debt in full. The balance outstanding at 30 June 2014 was £8,436,416 (2013:
£8,436,416). The directors believe that the balance outstanding will be ultimately recovered.
