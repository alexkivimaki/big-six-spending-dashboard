Key sources of estimation uncertainty
Provisions and contingent liabilities for player transactions
Creditors and provisions contain allowances for certain contingent amounts payable to players and to other
clubs based on management's best estimate of certain future events, such as the number of player appearances,
and the amount that will become payable as a result. Actual future costs may differ from the amounts provided.
Claim in respect of historic property developments
A subsidiary company is monitoring certain historic property matters to determine the basis of any
responsibility and whether they will give rise to any costs. As such any obligation cannot be measured with
sufficient reliability.
