The Group has commenced development projects to enhance the facilities at both its first team and academy training grounds
and the costs of these projects, where not yet completed, are included within assets in the course of construction as at the
balance sheet date. Completed works are reclassified to the appropriate fixed asset categories.

during the periods when Emirates Stadium was under construction. The capitalisation of interest ceased in 2006 when
